## Concept
Horror delivery game. Players load a delivery van (the "Graveyard Express") at a warehouse start area, then drive
it down a forest road to deliver packages to houses.

## Systems
- **Delivery chute** (`ServerScriptService.DeliveryChute`): when the van's rear doors are open and the chute mouth is
  inside the cargo area, a box spawns every 1.2s up to 24. Landed boxes are welded to `Cargo.Floor` after 3s or
  destroyed if they missed. Status lamp: red = no van, orange = doors closed, green = loading, blue = full.
- **Shops** (`ServerScriptService.StartAreaShops`): triggering a `ShopPrompt` fires `ReplicatedStorage.Remotes.OpenShop`
  to that client with the ShopId. **The shop UI is not built yet** (no client listener).
- **Framework** (`ServerScriptService.Server`): Bootstrap, Core (ServiceList, Scheduler, Net), Services (DataService,
  MonetizationService, ReplicationService, AntiExploitService). Persistence via `ProfileStore`. Admin via `Cmdr`.
  Shared code in `ReplicatedStorage.Shared` (Loader, Enums, Types, Util, NetDefs, Config).
- **Advanced Sprinting, Stamina & Exhaustion** (`SprintService` / `SprintController` / `ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Config.Sprint`):
  hold `[LeftShift]` / `[RightShift]` / Gamepad `[L3]` (or mobile touch button) on foot to sprint (`16 → 24.8` studs/s with smooth acceleration/deceleration, respecting `PersonalUpgradeSpeed`, `CarryingPackage` weight/drain, `AdminWalkSpeed`, and `AdminFly`). Drains `18/s` only while moving (`>= 4` studs/s; `1.25×` drain when carrying a package; `5` stamina per jump), regenerates after `1.2s` delay (`14/s` walking, `21/s` standing still or seated in `DeliveryVan`). Draining stamina all the way to `0` triggers **Exhaustion** (`IsExhausted`): locks sprinting, slows walk speed to `0.72×` (`~11.5` studs/s), imposes a `2.0s` recovery delay + slower `0.6×` breath recovery, tightens camera FOV (`-3°`) with a dark rhythmic vignette pulse + heartbeat/panting audio, and requires recovering past **`35%`** before sprinting is allowed again. Drives the Blender-authored R6 `R6_Walk` & `R6_Run` `KeyframeSequence`s smoothly across all co-op players via `Motor6D.Transform` (yielding arms to `CarryAnimController` and seated poses to `VanAnimController`) and renders an auto-fading retro-amber Stamina Bar HUD (`SprintStaminaGui`) above the hotbar with trailing drain bar, leading slider thumb, and `35%` recovery notch.
- **Unified Retro-Amber Terminal UI System** (`ReplicatedStorage.Shared.UITheme`):
  centralized `--!strict` design & animation system styling every 2D on-screen HUD, modal, and monster alert banner (`ShiftHUD`, `SprintStaminaGui`, `VanUpgradesGui`, `PersonalUpgradesGui`, `VanControlsHint`, `VanSeatHint`, `VanSpeedometer`, `DoorMonsterHUD`, `WindowMonsterFX` hint, `ChaseMonsterFX` pursuit bar, `CargoMonsterHUD`, and `NotificationUI`) with razor-sharp `0px` obsidian-black panels, subtle CRT depth gradients, L-shaped amber corner reticle brackets, solid-amber mini header strips (`>> GRAVEYARD EXPRESS // DISPATCH`, `>> STAMINA`, `>> TELEMETRY`, crimson `>> CRITICAL THREAT // PURSUIT`) with compartmentalized bordered metric cells, smoothly blinking crimson CRT status cursors, calibrated flat progress sliders with vertical leading thumbs, fluid sinusoidal animated flanking `>> <<` / `>>> <<<` button & alert chevrons, smooth `Quint.Out` hover fill sweeps, subtle hover/press scaling, smooth modal pop-in & row-hover glows, and preloaded official Roblox UI Hover/Select SFX — while leaving all 3D van cab buttons/dashboard/tracker screens, circular `[E]` interaction prompts (`PromptUIController`), `Cmdr`, and `HorrorAtmosphereOverlay` untouched.
- **Adaptive Eerie Horror Soundtrack & 3D Co-op Van Driving Audio** (`AudioController` + `VanAudioController`):
  - **`AudioController`:** Plays a rotating nocturnal eerie horror soundtrack (`Unsettling Atmosphere (b)` ↔ `The Plot Thickens Underscore`), low ambient dread drone, ghostly outdoor forest wind, sparse distant reverb-washed horror stingers, and an adaptive high-tension chase score (`Stalking the Prey 60` + `Panic Attack Drone`) that smoothly swells based on all 11 active monster threats and final `< 35s` shift urgency, softens inside the Factory Depot (`Prep`) and closed cab, and ducks into eerie stillness when `ForestHunter` stalks unobserved.
  - **`VanAudioController`:** Client-only 3D spatial `Attachment` emitters on `DeliveryVan.Chassis.Chassis` (zero parts/welds) driving ignition startup/shutdown, layered idle + heavy V8 load synced to `DashController`'s 5-gear RPM curve with gear-shift dips and `UpgEngine` low-end warmth, stall sputter on `VanStunned`, grounded wet-asphalt vs. off-road tire rolling hum, drift/handbrake/hard-brake tire screech, turbo `[Q]` Boost roar + blow-off release (both local driver and remote co-op), and a dynamic cabin `EqualizerSoundEffect` that muffles inside the closed van and opens up when rolling/breaking windows, leaning out, opening doors, toggling `[V]` chase cam, or standing outside.


## Planned: Core Loop
- Each shift is a list of deliveries. The van's in-cab `TrackerScreen` runs a real-time **Heading-Up GPS radar** (van chevron fixed pointing up, rotating `N / E / S / W` compass labels, rotating radar sweep): the current active delivery house appears as a bright pulsing **green dot**, remaining shift stops appear as smaller/dimmer green dots, and once deliveries finish or time expires the Next Factory Depot appears as a pulsing **gold dot**.
- The player is always behind schedule, and every monster threat costs time, so they choose between speed and safety.
- **Endless Forward Factory Depot Progression:** Instead of teleporting back to `(0, 0)` when a shift ends, both finishing all deliveries AND running out of time (`0:00`, forfeiting the on-time bonus) spawn/relocate the Factory Depot further down the continuing procedural road onto a reserved roadside lot (`~6–8` cells ahead) with a glowing green bay beacon. Players drive to the new roadside depot to collect shift payout, restock boxes at the chute, and browse shops. An invisible collision wall paired with subtle weathered industrial sawhorse barricades, blinking amber hazard beacons, and traffic cones blocks continuing further down the road until **every currently alive player** clicks the small **"Start Next Shift"** HUD ready-up button (automatically handling deaths, respawns, and disconnects).
- At the end of each shift the player is paid based on deliveries completed and time taken, then spends the money on van upgrades.

## Planned: Procedural Map
- The map is generated as the player drives. Roads, intersections and houses spawn ahead of the van; old chunks behind it despawn or are pooled to stay memory-safe.
- Uses the existing HorrorKit models in `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.MapModels` (Houses, Infrastructure, Props, Roads). Do not generate placeholder models; ask for missing ones.
- **Multi-Path Looping Road Network & Advanced Macro Formations:** Uses 11 active HorrorKit road templates (`Road_Straight`, `Road_Cracked`, `Road_Turn90`, `Road_TIntersection`, plus the 7 Blender road models in `Assets/Roads/`: `Road_Bridge`, `Road_PulloffBay`, `Road_DamagedRough`, `Road_CrossIntersection`, `Road_Roundabout`, `Road_SCurve`, `Road_WideTurn90`; `Road_CulDeSac` is excluded so there are zero dead ends). Randomly rotates through 6 distinct 100%-looping macro formations (preventing back-to-back repeats) while keeping all actual delivery stop houses strictly on straight main-spine cells and using existing `HorrorKit_Houses` (`House_A_Cabin`, `House_B_Suburban`, `House_C_Ranch`) strictly as non-delivery decorative houses with yard fences/mailboxes along side branches:
  1. **Deep-Forest Wing ("Out-and-Back Sweep"):** Shoots `4–5` cells (`400–500` studs) sideways deep into the dense woods around sweeping `Road_WideTurn90` bends and returns straight to the main road.
  2. **Staggered / Asymmetrical "Z-Split":** Offset dual-side branches where one side splits early and rejoins mid-way while the opposite side splits 3 cells later and rejoins further north.
  3. **Ghost-Town "Crossroads Village Block":** Compact `5×5` mini-neighborhood built around alternating `Road_Roundabout` and `Road_CrossIntersection` hubs with parallel side streets, 3 cross-alleys, streetlamps, and `4–6` decorative abandoned houses.
  4. **Long Forest Backroad with Mid-Way Bailouts:** `9`-cell-long forest route `300` studs out with sweeping `Road_WideTurn90` bends, isolated cabins, and a mid-way cross-connector bridge lane back to the main spine.
  5. **Double-Roundabout "Figure-8 / S-Hub":** Two asymmetric dual-loop roundabout lobes separated by a center `Road_Bridge` straight stretch with alternating wide (`3`-cell) and tight (`2`-cell) sweeping outer arcs.
  6. **Sweeping S-Curve Chicane Split:** Fast curvy outer route chaining `Road_WideTurn90` + `Road_SCurve` + `Road_WideTurn90` against a shorter `Road_DamagedRough` / `Road_Cracked` inner spine road.
- **Dense Deep Forest, Haunted Clearings & Roadside/On-Road Props:** Surrounding forest extends 4 cells (`400` studs) deep on every side of the road and grows progressively denser and taller farther from the road (`~0.88x–1.12x` roadside → `~1.50x–2.00x` deep woods; gently trimmed to `4–6` / `8–11` / `11–15` / `12–15` trees per chunk across Rings 1–4 and `175` around the starting factory for smoother FPS without looking empty) using 8 low-poly dead tree variants (`Prop_DeadTree_A/B` + `DeadTree_01_Fork`..`DeadTree_06_Crook`, source files in `Assets/Props/DeadTrees.blend` & `DeadTrees.glb`). Near-road trees (`< 95` studs from road center) remain non-collidable so the van never snags; deeper forest trees (`>= 95` studs) are solid blockers and outer Ring 4 edges are sealed with invisible `ForestBoundary` walls to slow down and contain players who wander off-road. `ChunkBuilder` also streams all **15 full-PBR horror decoration props** (`Assets/Props/ForestProps.blend` & `.glb`, pooled with `SurfaceAppearance` preserved): (1) forest floor clutter (`Prop_Boulder_Mossy`, `Prop_RockCluster_Small`, `Prop_FallenLog_Rotted`, `Prop_TreeStump_Old`, `Prop_BrambleBush_Dead`) scattered across forest rings; (2) rare creepy clearings in Rings 1–2 (`Prop_AbandonedWell`, `Prop_OldGravestones`, `Prop_WoodsTotem`, `Prop_AbandonedCampfire`, `Prop_HuntingBlind_Rotted`) with automatic 17-stud tree-free clearing rings and edge brambles/rocks; (3) roadside shoulders, house yards, and pull-off bays (`Prop_WarningSign_Rusted`, `Prop_RoadMarker_Post`, `Prop_ScrapTires_Pallet`, `Prop_RustedBarrels`, `Prop_CarWreck_Rusted`); and (4) solid **on-road crashed cars (`Prop_CarWreck_Rusted`) & barrel obstacles (`Prop_RustedBarrels`)** on both main roads (`14%`) and sidepath roads (`24%`), always hugging one outer lane (`±14.5–20` studs) so `>= 28` studs of open asphalt remain to swerve around cleanly.
- **Nocturnal Horror Lighting, Long-Throw Headlights, Tactical `[F]` Flashlight, VHS Static & Dynamic Rain:** Deep midnight lighting (`ClockTime = 0.0`, `Brightness = 0.68`, `ExposureCompensation = 0.14`, `Ambient = (18, 25, 31)`, `OutdoorAmbient = (30, 42, 52)`, `GlobalShadows = true`) paired with tighter cold dark-slate silhouette fog (`FogColor = (28, 42, 52)`, `FogStart = 20`, `FogEnd = 185`, tightening to `152` during Chaser storms, plus `HorrorGrade` contrast/desaturation and `Bloom`) keeps the immediate road readable in first person while hiding distant trees and houses in thick mist and leaving `StartArea` warmly lit by `WarehouseLights`. `DeliveryVan` headlights feature multi-stage ~145-stud long-throw warm-white beams (6 `SpotLight`s across `HeadlightL/R` shadow-casting near beams + massless `HeadlightMidRelay` / `HeadlightFarRelay` relays, zero untextured rectangular `Beam` artifacts, all toggled together via `[L]`). Players have an infinite-battery tactical `[F]` flashlight (`FlashlightService` / `FlashlightController`, drop-in ready placeholder `Tool` with `Handle` + `LensAttachment` at `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.Tools.Flashlight`) with upgraded crisp optics (`26°` `9.0`-brightness shadow-casting hotspot + `68°` cool-blue outer spill halo + `30°` far relay + foreground bounce `PointLight`, zero rectangular `Beam` box), smooth spring-lagged camera aiming in 1st person and strict forward character facing (`hrp.CFrame.LookVector`) in 3rd person, ProSoundEffects click SFX, co-op replication, and eerie monster-proximity flicker. `AtmosphereCameraController` adds softly tuned fullscreen 24 FPS `EditableImage` VHS static snow (`0.966` calm → `~0.905` threat), faint interlaced scanlines (`0.976`), gentle drifting tracking bars (`0.985 → 0.925`), and a soft dark vignette (zero text clutter) alongside moderate, motion-sickness-safe camera motion. `RainController` drives optimized client-side 8-curtain depth-layered exterior rain (`4` close crisp curtains + `4` wider outer sheets) plus a dedicated close on-foot glistening rain-needle halo (strictly open-sky outdoors, zero drops inside `DeliveryVan` or under roofs), driven by organic swirling wind gusts that intensify into horizontal squalls during `ChaseActive` Chaser pursuits, alongside low wet-asphalt drifting spray mist, `28` pooled dynamic glossy reflective `Glass` floor puddles (raycasted strictly onto open-sky outdoor ground, smoothly spreading in, shimmering with live concentric raindrop ripples, bursting miniature footstep splashes when walked/sprinted through, and smoothly evaporating), and `34` pooled surface-raycasted expanding ground impact ripple rings + arcing crown-splash micro-droplets; louder 4-layer ProSoundEffects rain audio (`DrizzleLoop` `9112857056`, `HeavyStormLoop` `9112853286` surging up to `2.2` outdoors / `1.25` muffled in van during chases, `StormWashLoop` `9112856394` up to `1.4` outdoors / `0.80` muffled in van, plus dedicated muffled `InteriorMuffledLoop` `9112852671` up to `1.85` in van with `+6 dB` low-end roof-drum rumble during chases); larger outdoors-only refractive `Glass` camera lens droplets (`0.072..0.135`, pool of `38`: ~`10–14` in calm drizzle surging to `32–38` during Chaser storms, strictly `0` inside the van or under roofs); and **true 3D refractive `Glass` van window droplets** (`202` pooled flattened `Glass` beads + `72` pooled shrinking wet-wake micro-trail beads parented strictly under `CurrentCamera.RainSystem.VanWindowDrops3D` — zero parts/welds on `DeliveryVan`, updated via single-call `workspace:BulkMoveTo`) hugging flush on the interior-facing plane of all 6 glass window panes (`Cab.Windshield`, `SideGlassL/R`, `DoorWindow`, `RearWindowL/R`) visible **only** while seated in a van seat in 1st person, featuring real-time light refraction, organic stick-slip sliding, 3D teardrop elongation along travel direction, cornering inertia, driving aerodynamics, and a clear central driving sightline.
- **Creeping Trailing Dark-Fog & Debris Barrier ("No Turning Back"):** As the van advances down the procedural road, a creeping `BackFogBarrier` (~10–11 cells / ~1,000–1,200 studs behind `maxReachedSpine` & `maxReachedGz`, always safely ahead of chunk despawn and clamped ≥8 cells behind the active Factory Depot while the shift gate is up) seals the entire map corridor East-West (`3,200` studs wide across two `1,600`-stud halves spanning well past both forest boundaries so nobody can drive around it) with an invisible collision wall, layered vertical-gradient dark fog curtains (`UIGradient` seamlessly dissolving into the night sky), billowing dark `ParticleEmitter` mist clouds, fallen dead trees, and broken guardrail/fence/chevron debris (no HUD/radar bump warning — purely atmospheric). Chunks between the barrier and the van never despawn, and leftover `StartAreaGround` is removed when the Factory Depot relocates. If the player drives past an active delivery house and the trailing fog swallows it, that stop is automatically marked **MISSED** (forfeiting its \$100 payout and shift on-time bonus) and the shift advances immediately to the next stop (or Next Factory Depot) so it can never soft-lock.
- Generation is seeded so a shift can be reproduced, and always keeps at least one valid route to the current delivery address.
- Delivery addresses are placed on houses that exist in the generated map; the tracker screen updates as new sections generate.
- Generation must stay compatible with monsters (e.g. avoid dead ends right behind the player so the chaser has a path).

## Planned: Monsters (each forces a different action)
1. **Window monster (`Monster2_Window`):** pops up at the left or right window. Player must close the window or the driver cannot see.
2. **Stare monster (`Monster3_Stare`):** appears in the rear-view mirror or on the passenger seat. Player must look at it until it vanishes, but looking away from the road makes the van drift.
3. **Light/screen gremlin (`Monster4_Gremlin`):** small crouched shadow-gremlin that perches on the hood/wipers or clings near dashboard electronics to kill headlights or the tracker screen. Player must fix it (a switch or a camera feed) while navigating blind.
4. **Chaser (Colossal Broken-Legged Skinwalker Crawler / `Monster6_Skinwalker`):** a huge, broken-legged crawler dragging itself behind the van via environment-grabbing IK arms; spawns **only** if players stand still for too long (`22s`), take too long without delivering (`70s` or `<= 35s` shift time left), or finish the shift with undelivered packages (`Return` phase with incomplete stops). Gets closer when the player slows down or crashes, falls back when they use Boost `[Q]` or `[H]` Horn flinch.
5. **Cargo monster (`Monster5_Cargo`):** twisted, spider-like stowaway crawler that sneaks into the back of the van. Player must check the rear camera and lock the cargo door, or packages get wrecked.
6. **Skinwalker (`Monster6_Skinwalker`):** uncanny humanoid/deer-like hybrid that stands motionless in the middle of the road to bait drivers into swerving—or transforms into a relentless stalker if hit.
7. **Forest Hunter / Deep-Forest Boundary Herder (`ForestHunter` — implemented v1):** a tall (~11.5 studs), moss-shrouded antlered Wendigo/Leshy entity with piercing Neon eyes (amber-white while stalking, flaring crimson on aggro) and elongated blood-red claws. Triggers only when an on-foot player wanders **deep into the woods** (`> 180` studs from both the nearest procedural road cell and `Workspace.DeliveryVan`, and outside `StartArea`). While stalking (`85–115` studs out), rain/ambient audio ducks toward silence and nearby flashlights flicker; if unobserved for `4.5s` it creeps `22–28` studs closer into the player's forward walking path with a subtle twig-snap cue. Looking directly at it (`Camera.CFrame.LookVector` dot `> 0.65`, or `> 0.58` with flashlight on, unobstructed raycast) or closing within `32` studs triggers a guttural screech and a `24` studs/s charge. Landing a claw swipe deals `-30` HP and applies directional herding knockback launching the player back toward the nearest road/van. Immediately retreats and dissolves back into the fog once the player reaches the road (`<= 42` studs), van (`<= 35` studs), `StartArea`, or a vehicle seat. Pairs with the dense, solid deep forest and the outer `ForestBoundary` walls.
8. **Door-Ripper (`Monster3_DoorRipper` / `DoorMonster`):** driver-side roadside hijacker that sprints out of the dark shoulder while the van is moving (`>= 24` studs/s), leaps onto the left running board, and pries the driver door open. The driver must mash `[E]` / `[X]` to pull the door shut (`DoorMash` remote) — slamming it shut knocks the monster tumbling down the road, whereas failing lets it rip the driver out onto the asphalt (`-35` HP, `-10s` shift penalty) while the driverless van keeps rolling forward (`VanCoastUntil`). **Fight animations:** during the struggle the driver lets go of the wheel, braces toward the door and hauls it shut with both hands (hands follow the swinging door, torso leans further out as it is pried open, head tracks the monster), and every `[E]` mash throws a punch or kick at the monster (right punch / left kick / left punch / right kick in rotation, visible to all players). The monster staggers on every blow (head snap, torso recoil, grip slip, screeching jaw, eyes flare, dark splatter) and claws/heaves at the door between hits; slamming the door shut ends with a final fist-and-boot shove.
